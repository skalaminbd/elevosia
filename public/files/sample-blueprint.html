The goal

Replace four disconnected tools with one dashboard for the office, a simple phone view for caregivers, and a client portal families can see. The first win is covering open shifts in minutes instead of a morning of phone calls.

What's in scope

Scheduling and dispatch

  • Weekly board with open shifts
  • Auto-fill by skill, area and hours
  • Caregiver phone view

Clients and care plans

  • Client records and visit history
  • Care plan notes and documents
  • Family access to updates

Invoicing and reminders

  • Invoices from completed visits
  • Polite reminders that stop on payment
  • Export to your accounting tool

Weekly reporting

  • Visits, hours and revenue per week
  • Open shifts and expiring credentials
  • Monday email summary
